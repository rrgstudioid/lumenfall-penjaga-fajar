LUMENFALL — COMBAT FOUNDATION V3
PHASE CFV3-1 — GLOBAL BASE STATS & PHYSICAL ATTACK FOUNDATION

PURPOSE

Rebuild the global character-stat foundation before rebalancing
Adventurer, Warrior, Thief, or future Specializations.

This phase establishes the new shared stat baseline inspired by the
classic MMORPG / Flyff-style progression philosophy.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT rebalance Warrior skills yet.
Do NOT rebalance Thief skills yet.
Do NOT create Specialization skills yet.
Do NOT add Stun yet.
Do NOT add FP yet.
Do NOT change monster HP/DEF yet unless required only for diagnostic tests.

==================================================
1. CANONICAL PRIMARY STATS
==================================================

LUMENFALL uses exactly:

STR
VIT
DEX
INT

Do NOT reintroduce:

AGI
WIS
LUK
STA

VIT is the LUMENFALL equivalent of the stamina/constitution role.

==================================================
2. STARTING BASE STATS
==================================================

Every new character starts at:

STR = 15
VIT = 15
DEX = 15
INT = 15

These 15 points are BASE STAT.

They are not player-allocated points.

Internally preserve the distinction:

BASE
ALLOCATED
EQUIPMENT
BUFF
FINAL

Example:

Base STR:       15
Allocated STR: +28
Equipment STR: +3
Final STR:      46

Do not store everything as one irreversible value.

==================================================
3. STAT POINTS PER LEVEL
==================================================

Character gains:

2 allocatable stat points
per level gained.

If character starts at Lv1:

Lv1:
0 earned allocation points

Lv2:
2

Lv10:
18

Lv15:
28

Lv30:
58

Lv59:
116

Formula:

earnedStatPoints =
max(0, (level - 1) * 2)

Do not grant retroactive extra points beyond this formula.

==================================================
4. JOB CHANGE LV15 — STAT RESET
==================================================

At first Core Job change at Lv15:

return all PLAYER-ALLOCATED primary stat points.

Reset allocated component to 0.

Base remains:

15 / 15 / 15 / 15

Then player redistributes all earned points.

Example Lv15:

Base:
STR 15
VIT 15
DEX 15
INT 15

Available points:
28

If all placed into STR:

STR = 43
VIT = 15
DEX = 15
INT = 15

Do NOT reset:

equipment bonuses
temporary buffs
permanent non-stat progression

Only the player allocation layer.

==================================================
5. UNIVERSAL PHYSICAL BASE ATK
==================================================

Create one GLOBAL Base Physical ATK curve.

Melee and ranged physical characters use the SAME level-derived Base ATK.

No Core Job receives free hidden higher Base ATK merely because it is:

Warrior
Thief
Archer
Knight
etc.

Use:

BasePhysicalATK(level)
=
7 + floor(level * 1.1)

Expected values:

Lv1  = 8
Lv5  = 12
Lv10 = 18
Lv14 = 22
Lv15 = 23
Lv20 = 29
Lv30 = 40
Lv40 = 51
Lv50 = 62
Lv59 = 71
Lv60 = 73

This becomes the global level-derived physical baseline.

==================================================
6. IMPORTANT: BASE ATK IS NOT FINAL DAMAGE
==================================================

BasePhysicalATK must NOT equal final outgoing damage.

Architecture should become:

Base Physical ATK
+
Weapon Contribution
+
Primary Stat Contribution
+
Equipment Attack Bonuses
+
Buff / Passive Attack Bonuses
=
Physical Attack Power

Then:

Physical Attack Power
× attack/skill coefficient
→ defense mitigation
→ final damage

Do not mix these layers together.

==================================================
7. REMOVE UNIVERSAL STR × 2 MODEL
==================================================

Current generic model such as:

STR × 2 Physical Attack

must no longer be treated as the final universal STR rule.

STR contribution must become compatible with a future
WEAPON-DEPENDENT factor system.

Architecture target:

PhysicalStatContribution
=
Allocated/Effective Offensive Stat
× WeaponStatFactor

Do NOT yet finalize every weapon coefficient in this phase.

Create the architecture/configuration point so factors can later differ for:

1H Sword
Greatsword
Dual Sword
Dagger
Spear
Knuckle
Bow
etc.

Do not hardcode one STR coefficient globally into combat logic.

==================================================
8. BASE STAT CONTRIBUTION POLICY
==================================================

IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE:

The character's base value of 15 should be part of the character's natural
stat baseline.

However, avoid accidentally double-counting the base 15 through both:

BasePhysicalATK
and
large stat contribution.

Audit the current formula carefully.

Return a recommendation whether offensive stat contribution should use:

A.
effectiveStat × factor

or

B.
(effectiveStat - baseStat) × factor

Do not silently decide this without reporting the impact.

We want controlled early-game damage.

==================================================
9. VIT ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Remove the assumption:

1 VIT = permanently +10 HP at every level

as the only canonical model.

Prepare VIT for a level/job-scaled HP formula.

Target architecture:

Base HP
+
Level HP Growth
+
VIT-derived HP
+
Equipment HP
+
Buff HP

VIT-derived HP should be able to depend on:

Level
Job HP Factor
VIT

Do NOT finalize all Job HP Factors yet.

Warrior, Thief, Knight, Mage, etc. will eventually have different
HP efficiency from VIT.

VIT should also remain the primary contributor toward Physical Defense.

Do NOT include Stagger Resistance.

Stagger is being removed from LUMENFALL gameplay.

==================================================
10. DEX ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

DEX remains responsible for physical precision/agility-style secondary stats.

Target responsibilities:

Accuracy / Hit
Critical Rate
Attack Speed
Evasion / avoidance

Critical baseline target:

approximately:

10 DEX ≈ +1 percentage point Crit

or equivalent:

1 DEX ≈ +0.1 percentage point Crit

BUT:

do not count the base 15 incorrectly if existing base Crit already assumes
baseline stats.

Audit this.

Accuracy should eventually compare attacker and defender values rather than:

Accuracy = 90 + DEX

as the only final formula.

Evasion should move toward a rating/formula model rather than unlimited
direct percentage gain.

Attack Speed must remain cap-safe.

Do NOT create Flyff-style speed breakpoints yet.

==================================================
11. INT ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

INT responsibilities:

Max MP
Magic-oriented scaling
Healing-oriented scaling

Prepare Max MP as separated components:

Base MP
+
Level MP Growth
+
INT MP Contribution
+
equipment/buff MP

Starting design target:

approximately +3 MP per relevant INT point

but do NOT redesign Mage/Acolyte damage yet.

Remove the design assumption that:

1 INT universally equals the same fixed Magic Attack
for every spell.

Future magic/heal skills should support their own INT coefficients.

==================================================
12. FP — NOT YET
==================================================

DO NOT implement FP in this phase.

We are considering FP as a future physical-skill resource.

For now:

preserve existing resource behavior sufficiently for compatibility.

Do not redesign Warrior/Thief resource costs yet.

We will make a separate decision:

HP + FP physical classes

versus

HP + Mana universal model.

==================================================
13. BASIC ATTACK FOUNDATION
==================================================

Basic Attack must become the reference point for physical skill balance.

Create or centralize a clean Basic Attack calculation based on:

Physical Attack Power
× basicAttackCoefficient

Initial baseline:

basicAttackCoefficient = 1.0

before defense mitigation.

Do NOT add arbitrary high flat damage.

Do NOT give Warrior/Thief hidden Basic Attack multipliers yet.

==================================================
14. WEAPONLESS STATE
==================================================

Character without a weapon must remain playable and must not soft-lock.

However:

weaponless damage must be meaningfully weaker than equipped combat.

Audit current behavior.

Do NOT allow removal of the weapon to leave nearly identical damage.

For this phase:

report the exact current difference between:

unarmed
starter weapon
level-appropriate normal weapon

before choosing final unarmed penalties.

==================================================
15. DAMAGE LAYER SEPARATION
==================================================

After this phase, combat code should conceptually separate:

A. Level Base ATK
B. Stat contribution
C. Weapon contribution
D. Equipment modifiers
E. Buff/passive modifiers
F. Skill coefficient
G. Target defense/mitigation
H. Final damage

Avoid double scaling.

Example of forbidden architecture:

STR increases Physical Attack
→ skill separately multiplies STR again
→ weapon separately scales STR again
→ passive multiplies entire result again unintentionally

Audit for these cases.
