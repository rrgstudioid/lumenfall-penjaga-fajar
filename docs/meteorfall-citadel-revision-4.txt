OWNER REQUEST
Particles appear attached to the camera/walking character. Follow Frostfire
Highlands' particle system while retaining the existing fire grains.

CAUSE AND FIX
Meteorfall previously reconstructed every particle position from the hero's
position on every frame, including its vertical reference. This translated the
whole cloud whenever the hero moved.

The implementation now follows the persistent world-position and bounded
recycling approach inspected in lib/game/frostfire-weather.ts:
- Live particles integrate their own gentle drift and upward motion.
- Camera movement only changes the local recycling window. It does not translate
  live particles. Large camera jumps initialize a new local pool.
- Particles wrap at distant bounds and fade at the boundary and birth/death.
- Ground sampling is staggered; individual particles recycle above local ground.
- Existing orange color (#ff9b36), size (0.16), additive glow and quality caps
  (256 / 512 / 768 / 1024) remain. One Points draw call, no new texture or light.
- Ground footprints, Frostfire's implementation, population (294), equipment,
  combat, saves and other existing work are unchanged by this revision.
